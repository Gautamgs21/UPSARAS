How this engine works & assumptions worth checking
  • Two payment tables, two jobs. Table A (Total payments) is used only to work out the shortfall. Table B (Payment available for adjustment of penalty) is the only pool applied against the penalty and its accrued interest.
  • Shortfall & penalty. Amount paid up to the cut-off = sum of Table A payments on/before that date. Shortfall = Revenue Share Due − that amount. Penalty = your chosen % of the shortfall.
  • Penalty-applicable date. 15 days from the Audit Report signing date, or 15 days from submission of the Audited Statement of Revenue, or a custom date.
  • Interest on penalty — same engine as the main calculator. Interest accrues from the day after the penalty-applicable date, using inclusive month counting and monthly/annual compounding.
  • Interest becomes new principal. Unpaid interest becomes its own liability and starts accruing further interest from the 1st of the next month.
  • Rate / method schedule & moratorium work as in the main calculator.
  • Payment waterfall. Table B payments apply first to penalty principal, then to interest liabilities in FIFO order. Surplus is reported.
  • Print layout matches the SUC Assessment reference — three header boxes, a rate schedule box, four rounded stat cards, and two full-width blocks (Calculation of Penalty, Calculation of Interest on Penalty) that never split awkwardly across pages.
Figures are rounded to the nearest rupee for display; calculations use full precision internally.
